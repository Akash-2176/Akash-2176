## 🧭 How I work

- **Scope before code.** I'd rather spend an hour asking "what problem are we actually solving?" than a week building the wrong thing. You get a clear plan and an honest estimate before anything starts.
- **I like the chaos.** Messy requirements, half-built codebases, teams pulling in different directions: that's where I'm most useful. Bringing order to it is the part I enjoy.
- **Right person, right task.** Whether it's a 9-person police project or a student team at EzuraArc, I split work by what each person is actually good at, not by who's free.
- **Ship, then sharpen.** Get something real in front of users fast, then measure and fix. The 35% latency cut at Intellect came from measuring, not guessing.
- **Updates you never have to chase.** If something's late or broken, you hear it from me first.
- **AI is my pair programmer, not my architect.** I use it hard, but auth, data isolation and edge cases get a human review every time.
